Registration Cancellation Policy
Refunds for registration are available until Tuesday, October 31, 2023. Refunds requested after this date will be considered on a case-by-case basis. A $30 processing fee for cancellation will be charged for all refunds. All cancellations and requests for refunds must be made in writing. Please email your request to forum@diglib.org. If you need to cancel your hotel reservation, please contact the hotel directly. 


Registration Cancellation Due to Illness
Registration cancellation due to illness 48 hours prior to the event will be fully refunded, however, documentation may be requested in order to receive a refund. If you need to cancel your hotel reservation, please contact the hotel directly.
