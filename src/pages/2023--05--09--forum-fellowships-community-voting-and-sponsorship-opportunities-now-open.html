Forum Fellowships, Community Voting, and Sponsorship Opportunities Now Open

  • May 9, 2023

Apply to be a 2023 Forum Fellow  

The Council on Library and Information Resources is thrilled to announce that the 2023 DLF Forum Fellow application is now open. This year we are offering a limited number of fellowship opportunities, including our GLAM Cross-Pollinator Registration Awards, in order to welcome new, dynamic voices and valuable perspectives that will enrich our events and larger community. Find out more about this year’s opportunities and complete our lightweight application by Friday, June 9, at 11:59pm ET.

 

Community Voting Now Open 

Help shape this year’s event programs by casting your vote! Community voting is now open through May 17, and community members can review titles and abstracts and cast votes based on their interest in seeing certain presentations as part of our events this fall. The program planning committees for each event will use the community’s input to help inform its decisions about the conference programs.

 

Become a Sponsor

We’re excited to announce that sponsorship opportunities are now open for the upcoming CLIR events! Our events are a great opportunity to connect with a multi-disciplinary cross-sector audience of professionals in the digital library, museum, archives, and cultural heritage fields. Plus, as a sponsor, you’ll be supporting our mission to advance research and learning in the digital library sector.  We have a wide variety of sponsorship tiers, add-ons, and custom packages available. Explore opportunities on our Sponsorship page or through the Sponsorship Pack. 

 

Get Ready to Register 

Get ready to elevate your skills and expand your network – registration for the 2023 CLIR events opens this summer! Hotel booking information is coming even sooner, so keep an eye out for that. Booking at the events venue through our room block will guarantee the lowest available room rates. In the meantime, check out our Conference Venue and Hotel page. 


Want to stay updated on all things #DLFforum? Subscribe to our Forum newsletter and follow us at @CLIRDLF on Twitter.
