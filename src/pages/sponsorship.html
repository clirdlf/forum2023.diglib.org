Meet our 2023 Sponsors

We are thrilled to invite you to join us as a sponsor at the 2023 CLIR events: Digital Library Federation (DLF) Forum, Learn@DLF pre-conference, and NDSA’s Digital Preservation on November 12-16, 2023 in St. Louis, Missouri. 

These events are multi-day experiences for learning, networking, and skill-building, working to inspire, connect, and share successes and failures with a dedicated community of digital library practitioners. 

Sponsoring the 2023 CLIR events connects your brand to a multi-disciplinary cross-sector audience of professionals in the digital library, museum, archives, and cultural heritage fields, from project managers, technologists, and developers to administrators, archivists, and service providers. These events have welcomed professionals from art and cultural heritage institutions, non-profit organizations, government agencies, and more.

More About the DLF Forum

The DLF Forum convenes a community of deeply engaged, hands-on professionals seeking actionable and informative insights to inform decisions for their organizations. With over 20 years of Forum organizing experience, we know what it takes to drive value for our sponsors.

While the Forum is open to all, DLF has a robust membership community, consisting of over 160 organizations. Take advantage of the opportunity to meet this engaged community where they come together across these simultaneous events.

DLF Forum with Learn@DLF Pre-Conference – November 12-15
2022 In-Person Attendance: over 400
2022 Accepted Proposals: 94 

The Digital Library Federation has over 160 member institutions and the conference is open to the public. The events kick off on November 12 with the pre-conference Learn@DLF featuring hands-on workshops for attendees to learn new tools and exchange ideas with fellow community members. The Forum will follow on November 13-15 where professionals from academic, art, cultural heritage, non-profit organizations, and government agencies from around the country and abroad connect, collaborate, and share their work and experiences. 

NDSA’s Digital Preservation – November 15-16
2022 In-Person Attendance: over 180
2022 Accepted Proposals: 29 

NDSA is a consortium of over 276 organizations committed to the long-term preservation and stewardship of digital information and cultural heritage. The Digital Preservation conference, co-located with the DLF Forum and Learn@DLF, is a meeting place for its member organizations and is a venue for intellectual exchange, community-building, development of good practices, and national-level agenda-setting in the digital stewardship field.

We’re excited to present the 2023 sponsorship levels. Please don’t hesitate to contact us at forum@diglib.org if you have any questions. Thank you for your time and consideration in supporting these exciting events!
