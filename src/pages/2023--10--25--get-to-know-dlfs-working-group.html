Get to Know DLF’s Working Groups

  • October 25, 2023

Though the annual Forum is DLF’s signature event, our working groups collaborate year round. DLF’s working groups organize across institutional and geographical boundaries to collaborate and get stuff done. Working group participation is open to anyone interested in getting involved, regardless of whether or not you work or study at a DLF member organization.

In the weeks leading up to the 2023 DLF Forum, we’re excited to share a series of posts authored by DLF’s working group leaders to acquaint you with their work, and how you can get involved.

We’ll share a new post on the DLF blog every day through early November. Posts from the Climate Justice Working Group, Assessment Interest Group, and Technology Strategy for Archives are now available. Stay tuned to the DLF blog for more in the days to come.
